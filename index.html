Bench

One day a week, your phone sits out.

Bench puts a wall around every app on your phone for one 24-hour window each week. You pick the day and the hour. You pick the few apps that stay open. Everything else waits until the window ends, and then the wall comes down on its own.

No streaks, no scores, no charts. Bench is not here to motivate you. It is here to hold the line you already decided to draw.

How it works

Choose your window, for example Saturday 6 PM to Sunday 6 PM. Choose what stays open with Apple's own app picker. Everything else is shielded during your window. If you need out early, it takes a 90-second wait and one typed sentence, and then Bench is over for the week.

Private by design

Bench uses Apple's Screen Time framework. It cannot see which apps you use or which you chose. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. Read the privacy policy.
