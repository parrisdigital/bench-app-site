Email support@parrisdigital.com. Replies usually within two days.

Bench didn't shield my apps

Open Bench and check that Screen Time access is allowed. If you use another app blocker, turn off its Screen Time access in Settings, then Screen Time. iOS applies one blocker's rules reliably, not two.

I can't open Bench during my window

Add Bench to your "What stays open" list. Bench asks you to do this during setup.

How do I leave early?

On the Home screen during a window, tap Leave early. Wait 90 seconds, type the sentence, and Bench is over for that week. It returns at your next window.

Calls and messages from my family are blocked

Bench shields apps. Who can reach you is set by an iOS Focus. Create a Focus named Bench in Settings, allow the people who matter under People, and give it a schedule matching your window. Bench's setup walks through this.

How do I change my window or my apps?

On the Home screen, tap Starts or Stay open. While a window is running these are locked; changes apply after it ends.

How do I remove Bench entirely?

Delete the app. iOS removes its Screen Time rules with it. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
