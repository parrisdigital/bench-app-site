Bench Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026

Bench: Weekly Reset ("Bench") is made by Parris Digital. This policy is short because Bench collects nothing.

What Bench collects

Nothing. Bench has no account, no sign-in, no server, and no analytics. It does not use third-party SDKs. No data about you or your device leaves your device.

Screen Time

Bench uses Apple's Screen Time framework (Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity) to shield apps during the window you choose. The apps you select are represented by opaque tokens that Apple provides; Bench cannot read which apps they are, and it cannot see how you use your device. Those tokens and your window settings are stored only on your device, in Bench's private app group, and are deleted when you delete Bench.

What Bench stores on your device

Your chosen day and time, your chosen exceptions (as Apple's opaque tokens), whether you have set up a Focus, your appearance preference, and, if you leave a window early, which window you left. That is the complete list.

Children

Bench is a self-control tool for the device owner. It has no parent or child roles and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
